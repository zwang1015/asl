Graduate student and visiting scholar positions are available!

Graduate student positions are available in the Autonomous Systems Laboratory (ASL) in the Department of Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering at the University of Tennessee Knoxville in the area of control, optimization, and machine learning for advanced space, air, and ground vehicle applications. The ideal candidate should be self-motivated and committed to getting a Ph.D. degree. Candidates with experience in optimal control, numerical optimization, and machine learning are highly desired.

Candidates interested in these opportunities should contact Prof. Zhenbo Wang for more information about the program and application. Visiting scholars are also welcome. Please include an up-to-date CV in the email.
